Before you estimate

What to measure and note

Fence length

Measure each straight run along the planned fence line and add the totals. Include returns to the house or existing fence when they are part of the enclosure.

Height and gates

Note the desired fence height and every walk or drive gate. Gates, corners, and end posts need different hardware than a simple straight run.

Site conditions

Tell us about slopes, old fencing to remove, rocky soil, sprinklers, retaining walls, and access for materials. These details affect the verified scope.

How estimates work

From a planning number to a confirmed fence project

  1. Use total linear footage and the number of gates to get an instant starting estimate.
  2. Send photos and your project address so we can review access, terrain, and existing conditions.
  3. We confirm the fence line, height, gate placement, and materials before scheduling work.

Planning estimates are not a final contract price. Property lines, local requirements, utility locating, and site conditions should be confirmed before installation.
