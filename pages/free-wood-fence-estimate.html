Helpful details

What affects a wood fence estimate?

Length and height

Measure every planned run and note the height you want. A simple backyard run and a tall privacy enclosure use different materials and installation time.

Style and gates

Privacy boards, decorative details, transitions, and gates all affect the layout. Gate width and location are important to plan early.

Ground and removal

Slopes, corners, old posts, concrete, landscaping, and tight access can change how the fence is installed and what needs to be removed.

Three steps before you request your estimate

  1. Sketch the fence line and add up the linear footage.
  2. Take photos from both sides of the yard, including gates, slopes, and existing fence sections.
  3. Share your project city, ideal height, and timing so we can confirm a practical next step.

We recommend confirming property lines, neighborhood requirements, and utility-locate needs before construction begins. A walkthrough helps make sure the plan fits the site.
