Before you estimate

Exterior details to capture

Paintable area

Estimate siding or exterior wall area, then note separate trim, fascia, doors, railings, or other surfaces that need a different finish.

Prep and repairs

Photos of peeling paint, open caulk joints, damaged boards, cracks, or weathered trim help identify prep work before coating.

Height and access

Tell us about second stories, steep terrain, decks, landscaping, and difficult-to-reach areas. Safe access affects the project plan.

A practical process

How we refine your exterior paint estimate

  1. Enter the main exterior area and secondary measurement for a fast planning range.
  2. Send wide photos of each side plus closeups of problem areas, trim, or repairs.
  3. We confirm surface prep, access, product needs, weather window, and timing before work is scheduled.

Exterior conditions vary from one side of a home to another. A confirmed scope protects both finish quality and the accuracy of your quote.

Exterior painting FAQ

Common questions before painting outdoors

Can I get an estimate from photos?
Photos are a great start, especially with your address, desired work, and visible prep needs. Some projects still benefit from an on-site walkthrough.
Does exterior prep matter?
Yes. Cleaning, scraping, caulking, priming, and repairing problem areas help paint adhere and protect the surface as intended.
