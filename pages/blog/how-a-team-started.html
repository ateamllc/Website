How A Team Started: Our Story

McKay and Elijah talk about how A Team began, how it grew, and what we love most about the work.

By McKay Whiting and Elijah Updated: November 19, 2026 Estimated read: 5 min

How A Team Started: Our Story

Quick answer: A Team started with friends who loved building things and wanted to work for themselves. It grew through hard work, good teammates, and a willingness to learn on every job. Today A Team Property Improvement serves Utah County with handyman, painting, drywall, and home improvement work, and our fencing side, A Team Fencing & Outdoor Structures, has its own site.

We recently sat down for a conversation about how A Team began and what we have learned along the way. This is a lightly edited version of that chat.

Where did it begin?

McKay: It started in high school. I worked on construction jobs with friends, we learned a lot of good skills, and we had a great time doing it. Our boss told us that if we wanted to build something of our own, we should start a business. Later, I was working for someone else and realized I could do the same kind of work for myself. So I called up the friends I had worked with, and we started A Team while we were going through college.

Elijah: I came in a little later. I grew up around construction and spent my summers building custom homes. When I came down to school I started working for A Team, and I loved it. I have always dreamed of owning a business, so I pitched the idea of becoming an owner, and I got to join the team as a co-owner. It has been a dream to build it together.

Who built it?

McKay: I was the first mover, but it has truly been a group effort. Elijah has done a ton, and we have been lucky to have great employees who brought new skills and new energy to the crew.

What did you start out doing?

McKay: The original idea was to keep doing what we had done in high school, which was remodeling and similar work. We tried a few different directions early on, which is part of the fun of starting something new.

Elijah: When I joined, we were doing a lot of fences and smaller handyman jobs. We also tried working with property managers for a while, and that taught us a lot about what we enjoy most: home repairs and home improvements.

Why “handyman,” and why a different parent company name?

McKay: Our first identity was A Team Handyman Services, and we have kept it because it is how most people know us. The parent company is A Team Property Improvement LLC, which keeps the door open for whatever comes next. We have talked about larger remodels in the future. It is an open-ended business, and we like that.

How did you find the work you are best at?

McKay: Fences. A friend and I built them in high school, and wood fences are forgiving: if you make a mistake, you cut or buy a little more wood and keep going. We felt we could do them really well, so we did a lot of them, and then we added vinyl and other materials. Fences are kind of our silver bullet. You can read more in why fences became our specialty.

Elijah: I agree. And every new teammate has brought a new skill, so over time we added painting, drywall, and more. Painting taught us some good lessons about prep, which we share in painting prep lessons.

What is the best part?

McKay: Every job has a surprise, and that is part of the fun of being a handyman company. You learn something new on nearly every one.

Elijah: For me, it is the people and seeing the finished result. Homeowners love seeing a clean, finished job, and we love giving it to them.

McKay: Our first summer, we put our energy into getting good tools and a van so we could do the work right. That investment has paid off ever since. We keep our setup lean and organized, and we shared what we learned in the tools every DIYer needs.

What do you say no to?

McKay: We know our lane. Electrical and plumbing go to licensed trades, and we do not do structural framing. We do enjoy non-structural framing, such as non-load-bearing walls and window and door framing. See what a handyman can and can’t do.

Where is A Team today?

We serve Provo, Orem, and Utah County with handyman, painting, drywall, and home improvement work, and our fencing side has its own site: A Team Fencing & Outdoor Structures. Learn more about A Team or see what we do.

Frequently asked questions

Who owns A Team?

A Team Property Improvement LLC, owned and run by a small group of co-owners in Utah County, including McKay Whiting and Elijah.

What is the difference between A Team Handyman Services and A Team Property Improvement?

A Team Property Improvement LLC is the parent company. A Team Handyman Services is the brand most customers know, and A Team Fencing & Outdoor Structures is the fencing brand.

What kinds of jobs does A Team do best?

Mid-sized home repair and improvement jobs, especially fences, painting, and drywall.

What does A Team not do?

Electrical, plumbing, and structural or load-bearing framing. Those go to licensed trades. We do non-structural framing.
