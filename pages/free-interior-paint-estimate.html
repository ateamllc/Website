Estimate checklist

Details that make a difference

Rooms and surfaces

List rooms and note whether you want walls, ceilings, trim, doors, cabinets, or select surfaces painted. Room dimensions or wall square footage help.

Condition and prep

Tell us about nail holes, cracks, stains, peeling paint, caulking, texture, and minor drywall patches. Good prep supports a durable finish.

Colors and access

Share color changes, ceiling height, occupied rooms, furniture, and your timeline. Photos in natural light help clarify the project.

Interior painting FAQ

Common estimating questions

Should I measure floor or wall square footage?
Wall square footage is most useful for paint coverage. If you only know room dimensions, send those with ceiling height and we can clarify the scope.
Can you include small wall repairs before painting?
Yes. Let us know about nail holes, cracks, patches, or damaged areas; photos help identify the prep needed.
Where is interior painting available?
We serve Provo, Orem, and nearby Utah County communities. Check our service areas or send your address.
